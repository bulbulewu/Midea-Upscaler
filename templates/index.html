🚀 Media Toolkit Pro V3 — Contributor Production Manual

Welcome to Media Toolkit Pro V3, engineered exclusively by Munajat International for stock media professionals and high-volume contributors.

1. Video Mastery Rules (Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Getty, Pond5):
  • Frame Rates: Never submit variable frame rate (VFR) footage. Our engine automatically locks into standard broadcast Constant Frame Rates (23.976, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 50, 60 fps).
  • Duration: Microstock footage must be strictly between 5 seconds and 60 seconds.
  • Audio Policy: Unless the sound is essential professional Foley, stock agencies reject 95% of clips containing background mic hum, fan noise, or copyrighted music. Audio is safely muted by default.
  • Color Profile: All exported footage is tagged with BT.709 colorimetry and faststart MP4 atoms.
2. Image Upscaling & Resolution Thresholds:
  • Megapixel Rule: Shutterstock and Adobe Stock reject images under 4.0 Megapixels. Our 4x and 8x engines easily reach 8.3 MP (4K) to 33 MP (8K).
  • Print Density: All upscaled outputs embed 300 DPI EXIF headers, allowing direct buyer printing.
3. Layered PSD Guidelines for Freepik:
  • Transparent PNG artwork is converted into unlocked, editable Photoshop layers with full alpha channel integrity.

2. bKash / Nagad / Bank Order Fulfillment Process
  1. Customer sends payment to your Personal or Merchant bKash/Nagad number with their email ID in the reference.
  2. Open the Administrator Control Panel → License Keys Generator.
  3. Type their Email ID, choose the access duration (e.g. 30d or 1yr), and enter their TrxID in the memo.
  4. Click Generate License Key and click Copy.
  5. Send the generated key to the customer via WhatsApp/Email. Once entered, the key locks strictly to their PC.
1. Offline Token Signing Architecture

Every license key follows the format MTK-PRO-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. It is cryptographically coupled with the contributor's email ID and system timestamp. When activated, the engine validates the signature locally without requiring external cloud servers, guaranteeing 100% offline functionality.

2. Hardware ID (HWID) Device Lock

Upon activation, the app captures the workstation's unique hardware identifier (Windows Machine GUID + CPU architecture). If a user attempts to give their key to another person, activation fails with an authorization error. If a customer legitimately upgrades their PC, simply click Reset HWID in the Registered Users table.

1. Why Stock Agencies Reject Video Footages
  • VFR (Variable Frame Rates): Phone cameras and screen recordings produce VFR which Adobe Stock rejects instantly. Our engine strictly enforces Constant Frame Rates (CFR).
  • Audio Hum & Copyright: Ambient mic noise causes 90% of rejections. Keep audio unchecked unless providing professional studio Foley.
  • Colorimetry: High-definition stock requires BT.709 color tagging and +faststart atom streaming metadata.
2. Image & PSD Secret Requirements
  • ≥ 4.0 Megapixel Threshold: Shutterstock and Adobe Stock reject images under 4.0 MP. Our 4x and 8x upscale pipelines ensure ≥ 8.3 MP (4K) to 33 MP (8K).
  • 300 DPI Print Tagging: High-tier buyers filter for commercial print density. Our pipeline embeds 300 DPI into EXIF tags.
