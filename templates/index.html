Frequently Asked Questions

Technical specifications and stock contributor advice

Why does the app strip audio by default?

Adobe Stock and Shutterstock automatically reject b-roll clips if ambient background noise, microphone hum, or copyrighted music is detected. Stripping audio ensures 100% acceptance for video b-roll.

What is the minimum Megapixel rule for photos?

Both Adobe Stock and Shutterstock strictly enforce a 4.0 Megapixel minimum. Our 4x and 8x Lanczos engines easily elevate any image to 8.3 MP (4K) or 33 MP (8K) with 300 DPI tags.

How does the app maintain maximum performance?

The engine uses automatic multi-core threading (`-threads 0`), hardware-accelerated encoding (NVENC), and in-memory image buffers for zero latency.

Can I host Version 3 on Render.com for free?

Yes. The included Dockerfile and render.yaml blueprint deploy seamlessly on Render.com's free web service tier.
